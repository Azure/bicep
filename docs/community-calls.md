# Bicep Community Calls

The Bicep Community Call streams live on our YouTube channel, [Azure Bicep (@AzureBicep)](https://www.youtube.com/@AzureBicep). No invite or sign-up is needed to watch.

- **Watch live:** [aka.ms/bicepcc](https://aka.ms/bicepcc) takes you to the next livestream.
- **Topics and questions:** each call has a GitHub issue with the [Community Call label](https://github.com/Azure/bicep/issues?q=is%3Aissue%20label%3A%22Community%20Call%22%20sort%3Acreated-desc). Add topics there ahead of time, or ask in the YouTube live chat during the call (you'll need to be signed in to YouTube).
- **Presenting at a future call:** sign up at [aka.ms/BicepCCSpeakerSignup](https://aka.ms/BicepCCSpeakerSignup) at least a week before the call you'd like to present at.
- **Feedback:** [aka.ms/BicepCCFeedback](https://aka.ms/BicepCCFeedback) (anonymous)
- **Past recordings:** [Bicep Community Calls playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLIIBXt9cyNDmpx9p3W_5NjpXL8ttlFMVJ)

## Add every call to your calendar

Subscribe to this calendar to get every upcoming call, and any date changes, in your own calendar:

```text
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Azure/bicep/main/docs/community-calls/bicep-community-calls.ics
```

- **Outlook:** Add calendar > Subscribe from web, then paste the link.
- **Google Calendar:** next to Other calendars, select + > From URL, then paste the link.
- **Apple Calendar:** File > New Calendar Subscription, then paste the link.

To add one call at a time, use the links under each date.
